Tools built around Aven by the community. These projects are maintained
independently. See their repositories for setup instructions and support.

## aven-nvim

[aven-nvim](https://github.com/alxhall/aven-nvim) by
[alxhall](https://github.com/alxhall) connects Aven tasks to specific
lines in files in Neovim. Create and update tasks from your editor,
open linked files from the Aven TUI, and delete tasks together with
their document markers.

Read the [author's introduction](https://github.com/raine/aven/discussions/23).
